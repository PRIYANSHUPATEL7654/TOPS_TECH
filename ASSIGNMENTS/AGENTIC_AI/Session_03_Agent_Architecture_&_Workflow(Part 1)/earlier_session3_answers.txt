5. Memory use cases:
• WhatsApp short-term memory: retain the last few turns so “make it shorter” applies to the previous draft.
• Zomato long-term memory: remember opted-in dietary preferences such as vegetarian or no peanuts across visits.
• Spotify contextual memory: use the last song or playlist context to recommend a similar track. Give users controls to inspect, edit, and clear saved preferences.
