Refunds may be requested for missing, incorrect, or damaged items within 24 hours of delivery. The customer should provide the order ID and a clear description. Support verifies the order and may request a photo. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. A refund is not guaranteed for a change of mind after food preparation has begun. Orders can be cancelled without a charge before restaurant acceptance; after acceptance, preparation or delivery costs may apply. Delivery estimates shown at checkout are approximate and may change because of traffic, weather, or restaurant workload.
